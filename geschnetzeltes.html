Zurich Ragout

Zürcher Geschnetzeltes

Prep: 15 minCook: 25 minServes 4

Method

1

Prepare the veal

Slice veal into thin strips ~3–4 cm long, ½ cm thick — cut against the grain. Season lightly with salt and pepper, dust with flour, toss to coat.

2

Sear the veal

Heat 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet over high heat. Sear veal in a single layer for 1–2 minutes until just browned. Work in batches — crowding steams the meat. Remove and set aside.

3

Sauté shallots

Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining butter and the shallots. Cook 2–3 minutes until soft and translucent.

4

Cook the mushrooms

Add sliced mushrooms. Cook over medium-high heat until golden and their liquid evaporates, about 6–8 minutes.

5

Deglaze with wine

Pour in white wine and simmer, scraping up the browned bits, until reduced by half, about 3 minutes.

6

Build the cream sauce

Add stock and simmer 2 minutes. Pour in cream, stir well. Simmer gently (do not boil hard) for 4–5 minutes until the sauce lightly coats a spoon.

7

Finish with veal and lemon

Return veal to the pan and stir gently. Simmer on low 2–3 minutes just to warm through. Add lemon zest and juice. Adjust seasoning.

8

Serve

Scatter with fresh parsley. Serve immediately with Rösti, spätzli, or rice. Pair with a crisp Swiss white wine.

Chef's Notes

  • High heat for searing is essential — the pan must be very hot and batches small to brown, not steam.
  • Don't overcook the veal — quickly seared and briefly simmered only. Overcooked veal turns tough.
  • Simmer, never boil the cream sauce — a hard boil will make it grainy.
  • Make-ahead: prepare the sauce a day ahead; add the veal just before serving.
Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g veal loin or tenderloin, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp clarified butter or oil, divided
  • 3 shallots, finely minced
  • 300 g button or cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 150 ml dry white wine
  • 200 ml veal or chicken stock
  • 200 ml heavy cream
  • ½ tsp lemon zest & squeeze of lemon
  • Salt & white pepper
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • Serve with: Rösti, spätzli, or rice
