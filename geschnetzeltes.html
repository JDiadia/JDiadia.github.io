Zurich Ragout

Zürcher Geschnetzeltes

Prep: 15 min Cook: 25 min Serves 4

Method

1

Prepare the veal

Slice 600 g veal loin or tenderloin into thin strips, about 3–4 cm long and ½ cm thick — cut against the grain. Keep the meat very cold before slicing for cleaner cuts. Season lightly with salt and pepper, then dust with 1 tbsp plain flour and toss to coat.

2

Sear the veal quickly

Heat 1 tbsp clarified butter or oil in a large skillet over high heat until very hot. Add the veal strips in a single layer — work in batches to avoid steaming. Sear for 1–2 minutes until just browned but still slightly pink inside. Remove and set aside; the veal will finish cooking in the sauce.

3

Sauté the shallots

Reduce heat to medium. Add 1 tbsp butter to the same pan. Add 3 minced shallots and cook, stirring, for 2–3 minutes until soft and translucent.

4

Cook the mushrooms

Add 300 g thinly sliced button or cremini mushrooms. Cook over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until the mushrooms release their liquid and turn golden, about 6–8 minutes.

5

Deglaze with white wine

Pour in 150 ml dry white wine and let it bubble, scraping up any browned bits from the pan. Simmer until reduced by half, about 3 minutes.

6

Build the cream sauce

Add 200 ml veal or chicken stock and simmer for 2 minutes. Pour in 200 ml heavy cream and stir well. Let the sauce simmer gently (do not boil hard) for 4–5 minutes until it coats the back of a spoon.

7

Finish with veal and lemon

Return the seared veal to the pan and stir gently. Simmer over low heat for 2–3 minutes — just enough to warm through and finish cooking. Add ½ tsp lemon zest and a squeeze of lemon juice. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.

8

Serve

Scatter with 2 tbsp fresh chopped parsley. Serve immediately with Rösti (Swiss potato cake), spätzli, or plain rice. A glass of crisp Swiss white wine — Fendant or Pinot Grigio — is the classic pairing.

Chef's Notes

  • Don't overcook the veal — it should be quickly seared and only briefly simmered. Overcooked veal turns tough and dry.
  • High heat for searing — the pan must be very hot before the meat goes in, and batches must be small, to brown rather than steam.
  • Simmer, don't boil the cream — a hard boil will make the sauce grainy. Keep it at a gentle simmer.
  • Substitutions: Chicken breast, turkey breast, or pork tenderloin all work well in place of veal.
  • Make-ahead: Prepare the sauce a day ahead; reheat gently and add the veal just before serving.
Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g veal loin or tenderloin, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 tbsp clarified butter or oil, divided
  • 3 shallots, finely minced
  • 300 g button or cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 150 ml dry white wine
  • 200 ml veal or chicken stock
  • 200 ml heavy cream
  • ½ tsp lemon zest
  • Squeeze of fresh lemon juice
  • Salt & ground white pepper
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Serve with: Rösti, spätzli, or rice
