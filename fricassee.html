Sweet Chicken Fricassee

with Fresh Pineapple

Prep: 15 minCook: 30 minServes 4

Method

1

Cook the rice

Bring 500 ml water to a boil with salt and butter. Add rinsed rice, stir, cover, and reduce heat to low. Cook 12–15 minutes until tender and water is absorbed. Rest covered 5 minutes, then fluff and keep warm.

2

Brown the chicken

Heat oil or butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add chicken pieces and cook until lightly browned on all sides, about 5–6 minutes. Transfer to a plate.

3

Sauté the aromatics

In the same pan, cook the diced onion until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1–2 minutes until fragrant.

4

Make the sauce

If using flour, stir into the onions and cook briefly. Gradually add broth, stirring continuously to avoid lumps. Bring to a gentle simmer until slightly thickened.

5

Finish the fricassee

Return chicken to the pan. Add pineapple chunks, cream, and curry powder. Stir gently and simmer over medium-low heat 5–10 minutes until chicken is cooked through and sauce is creamy.

6

Season

Season with salt, pepper, lemon juice, and sugar to taste. Add a splash of broth if sauce becomes too thick.

7

Serve

Spoon rice onto warmed plates or bowls. Top generously with the fricassee. Garnish with chopped parsley or coriander.

Chef's Notes

  • Fresh pineapple is best — canned works in a pinch but fresh gives livelier flavor and better texture.
  • Tropical twist: replace 50–100 ml of cream with coconut milk for a more exotic version.
  • Chicken thighs stay juicier than breast meat and are more forgiving.
  • Curry powder: start with 1 tsp and taste as you go — brands vary widely in strength.
Serves 4

Ingredients

  • For the fricassee:
  • 400–500 g boneless chicken breast or thighs, bite-sized
  • 2 tbsp oil or butter
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 1–2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 200–250 g fresh pineapple, cubed
  • 200 ml chicken or vegetable broth
  • 150 ml cream (or sour cream)
  • 1–2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tbsp plain flour (optional)
  • Salt & freshly ground pepper
  • Fresh parsley or coriander, to serve
  • For the rice:
  • 250 g long-grain or basmati rice
  • 500 ml water · ¼ tsp salt · 1 tsp butter
