Sweet Chicken Fricassee

with Fresh Pineapple

Prep: 15 min Cook: 30 min Serves 4

Method

1

Cook the rice

In a medium saucepan, bring 500 ml water to a boil with ¼ tsp salt and 1 tsp butter or oil. Add 250 g rinsed long-grain or basmati rice, stir briefly, cover, and reduce heat to low. Cook for 12–15 minutes until tender and water is absorbed. Remove from heat, leave covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork and keep warm.

2

Brown the chicken

Heat 2 tbsp oil or butter in a large skillet or sauté pan over medium heat. Add 400–500 g bite-sized chicken pieces and cook until lightly browned on all sides, about 5–6 minutes. Transfer to a plate and set aside.

3

Sauté the aromatics

In the same pan, cook 1 small finely diced onion over medium heat until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes. Add 1–2 minced garlic cloves and cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.

4

Make the sauce

If using flour for a thicker sauce, stir 1 tbsp plain flour into the onions and cook briefly. Gradually pour in 200 ml chicken or vegetable broth, stirring continuously to avoid lumps. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook until the sauce begins to thicken slightly.

5

Finish the fricassee

Return the chicken to the pan. Add 200–250 g fresh pineapple chunks and 150 ml cream (or sour cream). Stir in 1–2 tsp curry powder to taste. Stir gently and simmer over medium-low heat for 5–10 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce is creamy.

6

Season

Season with salt, pepper, and a squeeze of lemon juice (from ½ lemon) to taste. Add 1 tsp sugar if you'd like extra sweetness. If the sauce becomes too thick, add a small splash of water or broth to loosen.

7

Serve

Spoon the rice onto warmed plates or shallow bowls. Top generously with the sweet chicken fricassee and sauce. Garnish with chopped fresh parsley or coriander, if desired.

Chef's Notes

  • Fresh pineapple is best — canned pineapple in juice can work in a pinch, but fresh gives a livelier flavor and better texture.
  • Sour cream variation: Replace the cream with sour cream for a slightly tangier, less sweet sauce.
  • Tropical twist: Replace 50–100 ml of the cream with coconut milk for a more exotic, fragrant version.
  • Chicken thighs stay juicier than breast meat — both work well, but thighs are more forgiving if the heat gets too high.
  • Curry powder: Start with 1 tsp and taste as you go — different brands vary widely in strength and heat.
Serves 4

Ingredients

For the Fricassee

  • 400–500 g boneless chicken breast or thighs, bite-sized
  • 2 tbsp oil or butter
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 1–2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 200–250 g fresh pineapple, cubed
  • 200 ml chicken or vegetable broth
  • 150 ml cream (or sour cream)
  • 1–2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tbsp plain flour (optional)
  • Salt & freshly ground pepper
  • Fresh parsley or coriander, to serve

For the Rice

  • 250 g long-grain or basmati rice
  • 500 ml water
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp butter or oil (optional)
