Czech Tripe Soup

Dršťková polévka

Prep: 20 min Cook: 2 hrs Serves 4

Method

1

Pre-cook the tripe

Place the tripe in a large pot, cover with cold water. Add the peppercorns, bay leaves, allspice berries, and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer for 90 minutes until tender but with a slight chew.

2

Slice the tripe

Drain the tripe and allow to cool slightly. Cut into thin strips roughly 3–4 cm long and 0.5 cm wide. Reserve the cooking liquid if desired — it can enrich the soup base.

3

Sauté the onions

In a large heavy-bottomed pot, heat the lard or oil over medium heat. Add the diced onions and cook, stirring frequently, until soft and golden, about 8 minutes.

4

Build the flavor base

Add the garlic and caraway seeds to the onions and cook for 1 minute. Remove the pot from the heat, stir in the sweet and smoked paprika, then return to low heat and stir for 30 seconds. This blooms the paprika without burning it.

5

Make the roux

Sprinkle the flour over the paprika and onion mixture. Stir constantly and cook for 1–2 minutes to eliminate the raw flour taste.

6

Add stock and tripe

Gradually pour in the beef stock, stirring constantly to avoid lumps. Add the sliced tripe, dried marjoram, salt, and black pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes.

7

Season and finish

Taste and adjust seasoning. Stir in the white wine vinegar to brighten the flavors. The soup should be rich, slightly thick, and boldly spiced. Remove bay leaves before serving.

8

Serve

Ladle into deep bowls and garnish with fresh chopped parsley. Serve with thick slices of crusty bread. A cold Czech lager is the traditional accompaniment.

Chef's Notes

  • Bloom the paprika off the heat — paprika burns easily and turns bitter; pulling the pot off before adding it is a small but crucial step.
  • Pre-cleaned tripe from a butcher saves significant prep time. If using raw tripe, scrub thoroughly with salt and vinegar before cooking.
  • The vinegar at the end is traditional and essential — it cuts through the richness beautifully.
  • Tastes better the next day — like most braised dishes, the flavors deepen overnight.
Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g beef honeycomb tripe, pre-cleaned
  • 2 onions, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp lard or vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp hot smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1.5 l beef stock
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 whole allspice berries
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 1 tsp dried marjoram
  • Salt & black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • Fresh parsley, to serve
