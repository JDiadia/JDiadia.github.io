Czech Tripe Soup

Dršťková polévka

Prep: 20 minCook: 2 hrsServes 4

Method

1

Pre-cook the tripe

Place the tripe in a large pot, cover with cold water. Add peppercorns, bay leaves, allspice, and salt. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer for 90 minutes until tender but with a slight chew.

2

Slice the tripe

Drain and cool slightly. Cut into thin strips about 3–4 cm long and 0.5 cm wide. Reserve the cooking liquid if desired.

3

Sauté the onions

Heat lard or oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add diced onions and cook, stirring often, until soft and golden — about 8 minutes.

4

Bloom the paprika

Add garlic and caraway seeds; cook 1 minute. Remove pot from heat, stir in both paprikas, return to low heat for 30 seconds. This prevents the paprika from burning.

5

Make the roux

Sprinkle flour over the mixture and stir constantly for 1–2 minutes to cook out the raw flour taste.

6

Add stock and tripe

Gradually pour in beef stock, stirring to avoid lumps. Add sliced tripe, marjoram, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil then simmer 20 minutes.

7

Season and finish

Stir in the white wine vinegar to brighten the flavors. Taste and adjust seasoning. Remove bay leaves.

8

Serve

Ladle into deep bowls, garnish with fresh parsley. Serve with crusty bread and a cold Czech lager.

Chef's Notes

  • Bloom the paprika off the heat — paprika burns easily and turns bitter.
  • Pre-cleaned tripe saves time. If using raw tripe, scrub with salt and vinegar first.
  • Vinegar at the end is traditional — it cuts through the richness beautifully.
  • Tastes better next day — flavors deepen overnight in the fridge.
Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 600 g beef honeycomb tripe, pre-cleaned
  • 2 onions, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp lard or vegetable oil
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp hot smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1.5 l beef stock
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves & 4 allspice berries
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 1 tsp dried marjoram
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • Fresh parsley, to serve
