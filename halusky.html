Potato Dumplings with Sheep Cheese

Bryndzové Halušky

Prep: 20 minCook: 30 minServes 4

Method

1

Make the halušky dough

Finely grate the peeled potatoes into a large bowl. Do not drain — the starchy liquid binds the dough. Add egg and salt, then gradually mix in flour until you have a thick, sticky, spoonable dough.

2

Render the bacon

Cook diced bacon in a cold pan over medium heat until the fat renders and bacon is golden and crispy, about 10 minutes. Drain on paper towel; reserve the fat in the pan.

3

Prepare the bryndza sauce

Mix bryndza with sour cream until smooth and creamy. Season with black pepper — taste before adding salt, as bryndza is already quite salty.

4

Cook the halušky

Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Push the dough through a spätzle maker or large-holed colander so small dumplings drop into the water.

5

Boil and toss

Cook until dumplings float, then 30–60 seconds more. Remove with a slotted spoon and toss directly with the bryndza sauce. Repeat until all dough is used.

6

Combine

Toss gently until every dumpling is coated. Add a splash of cooking water to loosen the sauce if needed.

7

Serve immediately

Divide into warm bowls, drizzle with reserved bacon fat, scatter crispy bacon and chives on top. Serve at once — halušky firm up as they cool.

Chef's Notes

  • Never drain the grated potatoes — the starchy water is the binder. Most common mistake.
  • Bryndza substitute: blend 70% feta + 30% cream cheese if unavailable outside Central Europe.
  • Speed at service — have sauce, bacon, and warm bowls ready before the dumplings hit the water.
Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 kg floury potatoes, finely grated (unpeeled)
  • 250 g plain flour, plus extra
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300 g bryndza (Slovak sheep cheese)
  • 100 ml sour cream
  • 200 g smoked bacon or pancetta, diced
  • Salt for boiling water
  • Fresh chives, to serve
