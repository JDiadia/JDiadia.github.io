Potato Dumplings with Sheep Cheese

Bryndzové Halušky

Prep: 20 min Cook: 30 min Serves 4

Method

1

Make the halušky dough

Peel and finely grate 1 kg of floury potatoes into a large bowl. Do not squeeze or drain the liquid — the starch is essential for binding. Add 1 egg, 1 tsp salt, and gradually mix in 250 g plain flour until you have a thick, sticky, spoonable dough.

2

Render the bacon

Place 200 g diced smoked bacon in a cold frying pan and set over medium heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the fat renders and the bacon is deeply golden and crispy, about 10 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towel. Reserve the bacon fat.

3

Prepare the bryndza sauce

In a large bowl, mix 300 g bryndza with 100 ml sour cream and stir until smooth and creamy. The sour cream loosens the cheese to a saucy consistency. Season with black pepper — taste before adding salt as bryndza is already quite salty.

4

Cook the halušky

Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Place a spätzle maker, colander with large holes, or halušky board over the pot. Push the dough through the holes in small portions so little dumplings drop directly into the boiling water.

5

Boil and toss

Cook the dumplings until they float, then give them another 30–60 seconds. Remove with a slotted spoon and transfer directly to the bryndza sauce bowl. Repeat with remaining dough, tossing the cooked halušky with the sauce as you go.

6

Combine

Toss everything gently so each dumpling is well coated in the creamy bryndza. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of the dumpling cooking water to loosen it.

7

Serve immediately

Divide into deep bowls. Spoon a little reserved bacon fat over the top and scatter the crispy bacon and fresh chives over each portion. Serve at once — halušky firm up as they cool.

Chef's Notes

  • Don't drain the grated potatoes — the starchy liquid is what holds the dumplings together. This is the most common mistake.
  • Finding bryndza outside Central Europe: look in Polish or Eastern European delis. Substitute: blend 70% feta with 30% cream cheese.
  • Speed is everything at serving — have bacon done, sauce mixed, and bowls warm before the halušky hit the water.
  • Freezer trick for the dough — if the dough is very wet, chill it in the fridge for 15 minutes to firm it up slightly before pushing through.
Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 kg floury potatoes, peeled & finely grated
  • 250 g plain flour, plus extra
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300 g bryndza (Slovak sheep cheese)
  • 100 ml sour cream
  • 200 g smoked bacon or pancetta, diced
  • 2 tbsp salt (for boiling water)
  • Fresh chives, chopped, to serve
