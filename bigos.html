Hunter's Stew

Bigos

Prep: 30 minCook: 2–3 hrsServes 6

Method

1

Soak the mushrooms

Cover dried porcini with 300 ml boiling water. Soak 20–30 min. Drain, straining the liquid through a fine sieve. Roughly chop mushrooms.

2

Fry bacon and onions

Cook diced bacon in a large heavy pot over medium heat until fat renders. Add onions, cook until golden, about 8 minutes. Add garlic, cook 1 more minute.

3

Brown the meats

Add cubed pork and brown on all sides, 5–6 minutes. Add kielbasa slices and stir to get a little color on them.

4

Combine everything

Add sauerkraut, shredded cabbage, wine, strained mushroom liquid, canned tomatoes, chopped mushrooms, bay leaves, allspice, peppercorns, caraway, paprika, marjoram, and sugar. Stir well.

5

Slow cook

Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer at least 90 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add sauerkraut liquid or water if it gets too dry.

6

Season

Remove bay leaves and allspice. Taste — add salt, pepper, or sugar to balance. More sauerkraut liquid adds tang.

7

Rest and serve

For best flavor, cool completely and reheat the next day. Serve in deep bowls with thick rye bread.

Chef's Notes

  • The golden rule: Bigos tastes best on day 2 and day 3. Make it ahead.
  • Meat variety is key — add any leftover roast, venison, ham, or cured meats.
  • Dried porcini are non-negotiable — they give Bigos its signature earthy backbone.
  • Freezes beautifully for up to 3 months.
Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 500 g sauerkraut, drained (reserve liquid)
  • 400 g fresh white cabbage, shredded
  • 300 g pork shoulder, cubed
  • 200 g smoked kielbasa, sliced
  • 150 g smoked bacon, diced
  • 30 g dried porcini mushrooms
  • 2 onions, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 200 g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 150 ml dry red wine
  • 3 bay leaves & 5 allspice berries
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp dried marjoram
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
