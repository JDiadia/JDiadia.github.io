Hunter's Stew

Bigos

Prep: 30 min Cook: 2–3 hrs Serves 6

Method

1

Soak the dried mushrooms

Place 30 g dried porcini mushrooms in a bowl and cover with 300 ml boiling water. Soak for 20–30 minutes until softened. Drain, reserving the soaking liquid (strain through a fine sieve to remove grit). Roughly chop the rehydrated mushrooms.

2

Fry the bacon and onions

In a large heavy-bottomed pot, cook 150 g diced smoked bacon over medium heat until the fat renders and it starts to crisp. Add 2 diced onions and cook until soft and golden, about 8 minutes. Add 3 minced garlic cloves and cook 1 more minute.

3

Brown the meats

Add 300 g cubed pork shoulder and brown on all sides over medium-high heat, about 5–6 minutes. Add 200 g sliced kielbasa and stir to combine, letting the sausage get a little color.

4

Combine everything

Add 500 g drained sauerkraut and 400 g shredded fresh cabbage to the pot. Pour in 150 ml dry red wine, the reserved mushroom liquid, and 200 g canned chopped tomatoes. Add the chopped mushrooms, bay leaves, allspice, peppercorns, caraway seeds, paprika, marjoram, and sugar. Stir well.

5

Slow cook

Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for at least 90 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add a splash of sauerkraut liquid or water if it gets too dry. The longer it cooks, the better.

6

Season and balance

Remove bay leaves and allspice berries. Taste and adjust seasoning — it may need little extra salt as the sauerkraut and bacon are already salty. If too sour, add a little more sugar. If too mild, add a splash of sauerkraut liquid.

7

Rest and serve

For the very best flavor, cool completely, refrigerate overnight, and reheat gently the next day. Serve in deep bowls with thick slices of rye bread.

Chef's Notes

  • The golden rule: Bigos always tastes better on day 2 and day 3. Make a big pot — it rewards patience.
  • Meat variety is key — Bigos is a traditional "leftover" stew. Add roast beef, venison, ham, or any cured meats you have.
  • Dried porcini are non-negotiable — they give Bigos its characteristic deep, earthy backbone.
  • Freezes beautifully — make a large batch and freeze in portions for up to 3 months.
Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 500 g sauerkraut, drained (reserve liquid)
  • 400 g fresh white cabbage, shredded
  • 300 g pork shoulder, cubed
  • 200 g smoked kielbasa, sliced
  • 150 g smoked bacon, diced
  • 30 g dried porcini mushrooms
  • 2 onions, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 200 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 150 ml dry red wine
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 5 whole allspice berries
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp dried marjoram
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
