Stuffed Cabbage Rolls

Gołąbki

Prep: 45 minCook: 1.5 hrsServes 4

Method

1

Prepare cabbage leaves

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cut around the core of the cabbage head. Submerge and cook 3–4 minutes; peel leaves off one by one as they soften. You need 12–14 large pliable leaves. Reserve torn inner leaves.

2

Par-cook rice and onion

Cook rice in salted water for 8 minutes — slightly undercooked. Drain and rinse cold. Meanwhile sauté onion in oil until golden, 6 minutes. Add garlic, cook 1 minute. Let cool.

3

Make the filling

Combine ground pork, par-cooked rice, sautéed onion and garlic, egg, marjoram, salt, and pepper. Mix well with hands until firm and uniform.

4

Roll the gołąbki

Trim the thick central rib from each leaf. Place 2–3 tbsp filling near the base, fold in sides, roll tightly from the base like a burrito. Repeat with all filling.

5

Layer in the pot

Line the bottom of a large heavy pot with the reserved torn cabbage leaves. Arrange gołąbki seam-side down in tight layers.

6

Braise in tomato sauce

Whisk together crushed tomatoes, stock, tomato paste, sugar, salt, and pepper. Pour over gołąbki to halfway up the rolls. Tuck in bay leaves and allspice. Bring to a gentle boil, reduce heat, cover tightly, and braise 90 minutes.

7

Finish the sauce

Remove gołąbki carefully. Taste and adjust sauce seasoning. Remove bay leaves and allspice.

8

Serve

Plate 2–3 gołąbki per person with generous sauce and a dollop of sour cream. Garnish with dill. Mashed potatoes on the side are traditional.

Chef's Notes

  • Freezer trick: Freeze the whole cabbage overnight and thaw — leaves separate perfectly without blanching.
  • Don't fully cook the rice — it finishes inside the rolls, absorbing all the meat juices.
  • Roll firmly — loose rolls fall apart during braising.
  • Better the next day — sauce soaks deeper into the rolls overnight.
Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 large head of white cabbage
  • 500 g ground pork (or half pork, half beef)
  • 150 g long-grain rice, uncooked
  • 1 large onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp dried marjoram
  • Salt & black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 400 g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 300 ml beef or vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 bay leaves & 4 allspice berries
  • Sour cream & fresh dill, to serve
